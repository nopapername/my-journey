8 Unique Things to Do in Shanghai Only
Shanghai is a modern metropolis preserving traditional Chinese characteristics. The diverse and rich culture endows her unique charm everywhere and providing its tourists many unique things to do in Shanghai only. Below are top 8 of them to help you have a memorable trip in Shanghai. 
1  
Huangpu River Night Cruise
99% visitors to Shanghai will enjoy a cruise on its mother river, Huangpu River to see Western Style Building Clusters on the Bund and dense skyscrapers in Lujiazui. For a more unique Shanghai experience, you are recommended to take the Huangpu River night cruise. At night, the lights on both sides of the Huangpu River are shining and brilliant, making the buildings more attracting. It is the one of the most beautiful night views of Shanghai. In general, the duration of night cruise is about 90 minutes with the route of Shiliupu Wharf - Binjiang Avenue - Oriental Pearl Tower - International Convention Center - North Bund - Waibaidu Bridge - Western Style Building Clusters - Shiliupu Wharf. 
 Recommended Tour:  3 Days Shanghai Private Tour with Hotel from $509
 Huangpu River Cruise Booking
2 
Observation Deck in Shanghai Tower
As a metropolis abundant in skyscrapers, it must be a unique thing to do in Shanghai to climb up to one of them and have a panorama of the city. Observation Deck in Shanghai Tower, the world’s highest indoor observation deck, is one of the popular sites to gain such Shanghai unique experience. The observation deck is located at the 118th floor with the height of 546 meters (1,791 feet). Here, you can overlook the Oriental Pearl Tower & Jinmao Tower & World Financial Center in 360 degrees. After arriving at its bottom, you can directly take the express elevator to the 118F within 55 seconds. There is also a ‘Sky Post Office’ on observation deck, where you can choose characteristic postcards and send them to your relatives and friends. 
3 
'World Architecture Expo’ on the Bund
Due to its colonial history, the Bund gathers many western style buildings. Between the Waibaidu Bridge in the north and East Jinling Road in the East, there are 52 buildings of various styles, such as the Custom Building, Fairmont Peach Hotel and HSBC Building. Although these buildings came from different architects with different styles, they stand there one by one harmoniously to show the different beauty of Shanghai. Strolling along it, you are like visiting ‘World Architecture Expo’, being a very unique Shanghai experience. 
 Recommended Tour:  1 Day Shanghai Tour from $109
4 
Shanghai International Circuit
Going to the International Circuit in Shanghai will bring you unique experience in the whole life. You can watch world’s highest level F1 race. Every year, there would be more than 15 races from March to October. Lewis Hamilton from Mercedes AMG F1 Team, Daniel Ricciardo from Aston Martin Red Bull F1 Team, Fernando Alonso from Scuderia Ferrari F1 Team and other famous racing drivers are usually involved in the chase for the championships. If you want to experience the fun of being a racer and get close to the F1 racing motors, you can join in a test drive provided by Shanghai International Circuit. 
5 
Stroll along Old Narrow Alleys Lined with Shikumen Residence
When visiting Shanghai, the first impression of many people is high-rise buildings. In fact, Shikumen Residence in old narrow alleys is also an important part of Shanghai. Shikumen Residence is actually an old style Shanghai folk house combining architecture styles of southern China and western countries. It cannot be seen anywhere else in the world. It is also a good place to explore more profound cultural heritages and regional customs of Shanghai. Hence, strolling along old narrow alleys and watch Shikumen Residence is one of unique things to do in Shanghai. The areas gather most Shikumen residences include Tianzifang, Xintiandi, Bugaoli, Zhangyuan, and Jingyunli.
6 
Experience the Speed of Maglev Train
Maglev train in Shanghai is the fastest land transport tool in the whole world, and no doubt one of the unique things to do in Shanghai. With the highest speed of 430km/h (267 mph), it only takes about 8 minutes on the way between Shanghai Pudong Airport (PVG) and Longyang Road Metro Station, which brings a lot of convenience to passengers. The fast speed will leave you a deep impression and exciting experience. For tourists who landing at Pudong Airport, it is suggested to take Maglev Train to Longyang Road Station first and then transfer to metro line 2 to other parts of Shanghai easily, including East Nanjing Road and the Bund.
7 
Customize a Hand-Made Cheongsam
During the Shanghai trip, do you want to buy some special souvenirs? Then you can tailor-make a hand-made Cheongsam. Even after you go back home, this Shanghai unique experience will be refreshed every time you wear it. The masters of some shops in Shanghai have been making hand-made cheongsam for decades. From fabrics, workmanship and tailoring, each cheongsam is individually designed for each customer. Many hand-made cheongsam shops are located at North Shaanxi Road and South Maoming Road, the so-called ‘Cheongsam Streets’. Shanghai Shiliupu Fabric Market is a recommended choice for people who seek for reasonable prices.
8 
Overlook Night Scenery on Flair Rooftop
Flair Rooftop is a sky bar and restaurant, located on the 88F of the Ritz Carlton Shanghai Hotel. Here, impressive scenery and colorful night views of the Bund and Pudong area can be easily captured. When the weather is fine, you can sit on the outdoor terrace, blow breeze, enjoy the scenery with the company of the delicious food or a glass of wine, a very unique thing to do in Shanghai.  Read more:

Traditional City Center
Huangpu District, subordinate to Shanghai, is located in the southwest end of the confluence of Huangpu River and Suzhou River. It is close to Hongkou District and Yangpu District on the north, Luwan District on the south, Jing’an District on the west, and Pudong New Area on the east. It is one of the most prosperous and central areas of Shanghai where visitors can appreciate the wonderful cityscape. In addition, there are many Shanghai landmarks, old buildings and streets such as the Bund, Shanghai People's Square, Nanjing Road, Yu Garden and Shanghai Museum. It is better to spend 2-3 days to have an enjoyable Huangpu District travel.
Best Places to Visit & Top Things to Do in Huangpu District
 The Bund
Near to Huangpu River, the Bund is one of the must-see scenic spots in Huangpu District Shanghai with 33 buildings in different styles. Numerous skyscrapers that stand on the opposite side of the Huangpu River can be seen in the Bund, such as the Oriental Pearl Tower, Jin Mao Tower and  World Financial Center. At night, visitors can take a Huangpu River cruise at the Bund to enjoy the stunning night scenery.
 Nanjing Road
Nanjing Road is one of the ten famous pedestrian streets in China and the earliest commercial street established after the opening of Shanghai port. It is a shopping paradise as a lot of department stores gather here with a complete range of goods. Creative city sculptures are everywhere, and there are plenty of chairs in the middle of the street for visitors to relax. It's a good experience to ride a scaled-down version of the old tram which was the main transportation method in Shanghai before.
 Yu Garden
Built in the Ming Dynasty (1368-1644), Yu Garden is known as one of the five classical gardens in Shanghai with the exquisite pavilions, halls, rockeries, ponds and cloisters. The highlights of Yu Garden include the Great Rockery, Cuixiu Hall, Sansui Hall, Wanhua Chamber and Exquisite Jade Rock. On the outside of Yu Garden is Yuyuan Bazaar where visitors can buy souvenirs and taste delicious foods.
 Shanghai Museum
Shanghai Museum is a large museum of ancient Chinese art, with 12 special exhibition rooms and over 120,000 fine collections. The museum gathers historical relics of different dynasties, especially bronzes, chinaware, calligraphy and paintings. In addition, special exhibitions are often held in the museum, displaying various Chinese and foreign cultural relics, art works, calligraphy and paintings.
Best Time to Visit Huangpu District
The best time to visit Huangpu District Shanghai is March to May and October to November when the climate is relatively pleasant with the average temperature of 15℃ (59℉). Huangpu District China has four distinctive seasons. From July to August are the hottest months of the year and the highest temperature is 37℃ (97℉). December to February is the coldest period of the year with a temperature of 7℃ (45℉). The rainy season in Shanghai starts in the middle of June every year and lasts for a month. It is not a good time to have a Shanghai travel.

Transportation
Visitors can fly to Shanghai Pudong International Airport and then take Metro Line 2 to Huangpu District Shanghai or to Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport and then take Metro Line 10 to there. It is easy to reach there by bullet trains as the Shanghai Railway Station provides all kinds of scheduled trains.
Metro, buses and taxis are the main transportation means in Huangpu District Shanghai. The Shanghai Maglev Train also serves the public and visitors can reach the Shanghai Pudong International Airport in 40 minutes. Metro Line 1, 2, 4, 8, 9, 10 and 13 are available there. Visitors can take Metro Line 10 and 2 to get to the Bund and Nanjing Road. It is also recommended to take the Shanghai Sightseeing Bus for a one-day or two-day city tour. The starting price and charging standard of Shanghai taxi are different between day and night.

Shanghai Weather in November
 November marks the comfortable autumn time in Shanghai. During this month, the temperature is cool most of the time, the weather of Shanghai is stable with plenty of soft sunshine and high visibility, and the city is relatively quiet with not very big crowds, so you will have a satisfying travel experience no matter where you plan to go. Moreover, November provides stunning autumn leaves to see.  Shanghai in November  
Mostly Cool, with Some Cold Nights at the End of the Month Visitors in Shanghai in Cool November Overall, Shanghai in the first half of November is not cold at all, but quite mild and comfortable. On most days, the diurnal hours are perfectly warm or cool, with a temperature range of around 17-23 degrees Celsius (63-73 degrees Fahrenheit); the nocturnal hours are refreshingly cool at 13-16°C (55-61°F). Rare instances that the temperature throughout the day suddenly drops to around 5-10°C (41-50°F) may occur in some years.
Stepping into the second half month, the climate of Shanghai features cool daytime and a little chilly nighttime. Specifically, highs typically range between 10 and 20°C (50-68°F), hovering around 15°C (59°F) most of the time, while daily lows drop to around 5-10°C (41-50°F). 
Quite Sunny WeatherDuring your Shanghai trips in November, you can expect many sunny hours. On the one hand, Shanghai has 11 rainy days in November averagely, a relatively low level. On the other hand, rain this month typically lasts from a few dozen minutes to two hours, and rarely lasts all day.
In addition, even when it rains, you can usually still go out for sightseeing, because light rain and drizzle are the main forms of rainfall this month, while heavy rain and downpours are extremely rare. Street View in Sunny November  
A Little High HumidityIn November, the average humidity in Shanghai is 72%. On sunny days, the air generally feels dry and comfortable. However, on cloudy or rainy days, the damp feeling will be noticeable, and the apparent temperature will be about 2-3°C (4-5°F) lower.
Short Daylight HoursIn November, the sun rises in Shanghai between approximately 06:09 and 06:33, and sets early at 16:51-17:05. Accordingly, the Bund lights up at 18:00, and Yu Garden’s opening hours for night visits advance to 18:00-21:20. Daytime shortens from around 11 hours at the beginning of the month to around 10.2 hours by the end of the month. Therefore, be sure to plan your itinerary in advance to make the most of the period from 9:00 to 16:00, when natural lighting conditions are optimal.

What to Wear in Shanghai in November 2026?  Clothes for the First Half Month: Long-sleeved T-shirts or shirts plus a coat like a denim jacket, a sweatshirt jacket, or a trench coat are recommended to wear for the upper body. For the lower body, jeans and sweatpants are proper choices.
 Clothes for the Second Half Month: As the temperature drops further, you need to wear thicker clothes to keep warm. For instance, you can wear hoodies, leather jackets, plus sweatpants; shirts, 3-in-1 jackets, plus cargo pants; or thin knitwear, wool coats, plus jeans. Thin down jackets are advised to pack for cold nights.
 Shoes: Whether in the first or second half of the month, you are recommended to pack comfortable sneakers or casual shoes - November is the perfect time for exploring the city on foot.
One of the Best Months to Visit ShanghaiTrying to find a good time to visit Shanghai? November is a perfect choice, which features good weather, beautiful scenery, fewer crowds, and lower costs. Let’s be more specific:
1. As mentioned above, the temperature is cool but not yet cold most of the time, and the sky is most time clear and sunny with high visibility this month. So, no matter visiting the Yu Garden or strolling around the Bund, you will feel pleasant and see the beauty of Shanghai clearly. 
2. With no long public holidays, November is a relatively quiet time in Shanghai, when you don’t need to wait in long lines at famous attractions and won’t feel very crowded when taking the metro. Besides, the hotels are more affordable at this time.
3. November is the time when Shanghai is decorated with golden and red leaves, forming a romantic, silent, and unique atmosphere. Yu Garden with Fewer Visitors  
Tips and Recommendations for Visiting Shanghai in November1. Around mid-November each year, Shanghai’s autumn foliage reaches its peak, transforming the entire city into a colorful world. In addition to the Yu Garden, Gongqinglin Park, Century Park, and Lu Xun Park in downtown Shanghai are also highly recommended spots for enjoying the fall colors. If you visit Shanghai in late November, places like Sinan Road, Wukang Road, and West Lujiazui Road will be carpeted with golden fallen leaves, creating stunning views.
2. By the end of November, a lively Christmas atmosphere can be felt in Shanghai. For instance, Christmas trees and other festival decorations begin appearing throughout the city, and Christmas markets pop up in places like the Bund Source. Additionally, it’s worth noting that Disneyland Park hosts special Christmas events, including festival-related parades, Christmas tree lighting ceremonies, and Christmas dance performances.
3. Don’t miss Zhujiajiao in November. At this time, the water in Zhujiajiao is crystal clear, like jelly, and you can even see the swaying water plants and swimming fish clearly. Combined with the mild temperatures and fewer crowds, strolling around here is a very pleasant experience. Zhujiajiao Water Town in Autumn 
4. This is the peak season for hairy crabs, and you are highly recommended to have a try.
