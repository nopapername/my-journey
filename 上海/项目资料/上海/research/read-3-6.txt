Sinan Road
 Sinan Road is one of the best old streets in Shanghai to escape from the bustling city, with western style villas, small delicate shops and restaurants, in the shade of tall plane trees. It joins Huaihai Road to Taikang Road in the Former French Concession. Sinan Road was built in 1914 and named ‘Rue Massenet’ by the French Concession authorities to commemorate the famous French musician who died on August 13, 1912.
One of the Best Old Streets in ShanghaiExquisite houses and tall plane trees edge both sides of the road. In addition to the common road features in the Former French Concession, Sinan Road has more than 20 various old Shanghai style garden houses which makes it a unique attraction. Besides, Sinan Road does not have buses nor neon signboards. The street lights are old-fashioned. All of these make Sinan Road one of the best and most beautiful old streets in Shanghai.    
A Great Deal of Former Residences of CelebritiesThose garden houses used to be residences for celebrities. Former Chinese premier Zhou Enlai has lived at No. 73 Sinan Road, which is a Spanish-style garden house. Moreover, Chinese Peking opera artist Mei Langfang and Chinese modern democratic revolutionaries Sun Yat-sen and his wife Soong Ching-ling also have lived in Sinan Road. These residences are still well preserved and can be visited.  
Sinan Mansions - A Nice Leisure and Entertainment PlaceSinan Mansions are not one building, but buildings of 51 historic garden houses. They also bring together a variety of architectural styles. There are boutique hotels, apartment hotels and enterprise mansions. Moreover, there are a variety of restaurants, bars, coffee houses, tea houses, as well as a large number of clubs, convention centers, showrooms, cultural salons,  designer shops and recreational parks. Whether you are going shopping, enjoying deicious food, staying overnight or appreciating European style architecture, Sinan Mansions can cater to your needs.

3. Take metro line 10 and get off at Xintiandi Station, leave from exit 5 and walk almost 700 meters (770 yards) to the west.
4. Take metro line 1 and get off at South Shaanxi Road Station, leave from exit 4 and walk about 500 meters (550 yards) to the east.

6. Take bus line 26, 42, 167, 198, 320, 911, 920 and 926 and get off at Huaihai Middle Road Station.

Thames Town, Shanghai
 Located in the core area of Songjiang New City, Songjiang District, Thames Town is a large British style community. It is about 41 kilometers (25 miles) from central Shanghai. The town covers a total area of about 1,000,000 square meters (247 acres), with a total construction area of 500,000 square meters (124 acres). In recent years, more and more visitors flock to the town for relaxation, taking photos, or attending various exhibitions and activities.
When visiting the Thames Town Shanghai, you can't help but be attracted by the beautiful and dreamy town where streets, squares, houses, sculptures, streetlights and signboards just remind you of Britain. At the heart of town is a commercial area with many shops, restaurants, galleries, bars, cafés and wedding dress shops. The north, south and west of the town's commercial area are residential areas not open to tourists. To the east of the business district is a scenic lake where you can take a yacht ride or paddle around the lake.
Best Places to Visit & Top Things to Do
Catholic Church
The Catholic Church is the landmark building of the Thames Town. With the largest area, it’s one of the most beautiful spots of the town. Its appearance is like an English castle and characterized by the Baroque style. Surrounded by green spaces on three sides, the main body of the church has three spires, all of which have cross symbols. And the painted glasses of the church are extremely fancy under the sunshine. The church also attracts many new couples to take photos. It should be noted that the church closes early, so it is recommended to first hit when you visit the Thames Town Songjiang.
Zhongshuge Bookshop – One of the Most Beautiful Bookstores in China
Visitors to Thames Town Shanghai cannot miss the Zhongshuge Bookshop, one of the most famous shops in China for its exquisite layout and well-designed bookshelves. This English-style building with jujube red exterior wall perfectly integrates into the street view. Its glass curtain wall is painted with selected passages from classic books from all over the world. Once you enter this bookstore, you will be impressed by the books on the wall, the glass ground and even the glass ceiling. But the books on the ceiling are fake, just for decoration. The bookshop has two floors. The first floor is divided into nine rooms according to different themes. The second floor is full of curved white bookshelves. The ceiling is mirror-like, so is the backboard of the bookshelf, making people seem to fall into the sea of books. There are afternoon tea areas on the first and second floors. Sitting by the window, you can see the street view of Thames Town.
Songjiang Art Museum
The simple and elegant Songjiang Art Museum has three floors. In addition to a few geometric chairs with a strong sense of design for visitors to rest, the museum leaves space for artists' works to the maximum extent. The museum is open for free and invites different artists to hold itinerant exhibitions every week, displaying various works from avant-garde abstract oil paintings to traditional calligraphy and landscape paintings. In addition, there is a film salon on weekends. Most of the low-budget films produced by European directors are shown and people can spend most of the afternoon leisurely here.
Cruise on Huating Lake
Located in the east of Thames Town Songjiang, Huating Lake is a beautiful artificial lake with an area of 200,000 square meters (49 acres). Around the lake, there are houses, hotels, green lawns and viewing platforms. On the west bank of the lake is a wharf with yachts in line. A wonderful yacht cruise can be experienced here. The yacht goes north along the Shengjingtang River, passes Songjiang University Town and finally arrives at the Guangfulin Site of Ancient Culture, offering fancy river views along the way. Grand dragon boat races are held every year on the lake in Dragon Boat Festival, the 5th day of the fifth lunar month.
Sightsee around the Town by Bike, Train
It takes about 2 – 3 hours to walk around the Thames Town. The statues, signposts, red English architecture and shops with different characteristics can be seen along the way. At the same time, there are open-air tea seats, coffee seats and sunshade umbrellas, which can be used for tourists to rest. If interested, you can rent tandem bicycles, bicycles for three and a sightseeing train. The bicycle costs CNY60/hour.
How to get to Thames Town
By Metro:
Take Metro Line 9, and get off at the Songjiang University Town, Exit 1. Walk north about 100m to take the Songjiang Tram Line T2 and get off at the Thames Town West Station. Opposite the station is the Thames Town
By Bus:
Take Songjiang Bus 24, 19, get off at the Wencheng Road Yuhua Road and then walk east for about 3 minutes. Take Songjiang Bus 14, 15, 19, get off at North Sanxin Road New Songjiang Road, and then walk south for 4 minutes.

1933 Old Millfun
1933 Old Millfun, or 1933 Laochangfang in Chinese, or simply 1933, now in Hongkou District, was originally a slaughterhouse, built in the year 1933 at the intersection of Hongkou Port and Shajing Port, not far from the Bund. The architect the slaughterhouse was Balfours, a famous English designer. At that time, it was one of three largest slaughterhouses in the world. Nowadays, it has been transformed into a cluster of creative art precincts and has become the center of Shanghai’s fashion, design, shopping and entertainment. Dramas and fashion shows are often given here.
One of World’s Largest and Most Advanced Slaughterhouse When Built  
1933 Old Millfun, with an area of 31,700 square meters (341,216 square feet), is a reinforced concrete structure. The outer walls are about 50 cm (20 inches) thick, hollow inside. As equipment for temperature control was not available at the time it was built, usage of this construction approach skillfully controlled the interior temperature; even in a hot summer, it is cool inside.
1933 Old Millfun combines eastern and western styles. There is an outer square building with a circular core, based on the Chinese concept “the earth is square and the sky is round”. Viewed as a whole, it is in ancient roman basilica style. 
What to See about the Building 1933 Old Millfun
Lattice Windows
Location: Facade
Lattice windows face west, taking advantage of Shanghai's predominant wind direction and thus allowing for better natural ventilation. The windows also face the western paradise, which was thought to allow a better reincarnation for the slaughtered animals.
Umbellate Columns
Location: between the first floor and the second and a half floor
In the absence of beams, the whole outer building is supported by over 300 columns. The umbrella-like, umbellate caps of the columns are square or octagonal. Visitors can see them along the interior periphery of the square outer building. 
Covered Bridges
Location: the second floor and the second and a half floor
There are altogether four-storey of corridors and 26 sloping, bridging walkways in 1933 Old Millfun, connecting the central circular core with the outer square building. They form special spaces inside the building; together with the mysterious and varied architectural light and shadow, they are popular sites for photography. These covered bridges form a labyrinth, making this building a giant maze. 
Central Atrium
Location: first to third floors
This used to be the site of the main slaughtering floor. The central atrium is connected to the different floors of the outer building by spiral staircases and criss-crossing staircases. The spiral staircases are about one person wide with a graceful arc at a large angle and were designed to help workers escape easily if the animals were frightened and became dangerous.
Sky Theater
Location: the fourth floor
The Sky Theater covers an area of more than 1,500 square meters (1,800 square yards). The center-piece of the 8 meters high Sky Theater, with an area of 600 square meters (720 square yards), is a dome seemingly suspended in midair. It is made of toughened glass, transparent and dazzling. The round dome structure adds visual and spatial impact. The theatre is open only where there is a show, party or other similar activities. 
Things to Do in 1933 Old Millfun
Enjoy Delicious Food
One of the most recommended 1933 Shanghai restaurants is the Niushi Chinese Restaurant, located in Unit 1-203. It is famous for Shanghai and Cantonese dishes made from beef. After the main meal, one can proceed to La Douceur, in unit 1-105, to taste some French style desserts and coffee. Other themed restaurants are worth trying, including Fomula Pilota, themed on Formula One racing, and Canil, where the “waiters” are six cute dogs. 
Go Shopping in Boutique Stores
In Zhenxiangfang Hand Bead String Shop, the designers combine teaism, Tai Chi, Fengshui, and other Chinese culture references into every hand bead string. They also customize hand bead strings based on guests’ wishes. Many other creative clothing, ornament and furniture designers have also opened workshops here. You will find something new and special on each visit.   
1933 Mimi Theater 
Dramas and other kinds of stage shows are given here on an irregular basis. Due to the short distance between the stage and seats, the audience can have a different viewing experience. The theatre is in Unit 1-201. 
1933 Old Millfun Game House
This is a paradise for game lovers. Every visitor is given a private space to indulge their fantasies in the game world. 
How to Get to 1933 Old Millfun
1. Take bus line 854 and get off at Liyang Road - Zhoujiazui Road Station. And walk northward for about 200 meters to get there.
2. Take bus line 25 and get off at Wuzhou Road - Yuhang Road Station; walk northward to the road cross and you will see it. 
3. Take bus line 6, 13, 17, 33, 145, 220, 308, 319, 510, 939, and get off at Zhoujiazui Road - Xinjian Road Station. The building is to your northwest.

Shanghai Century Park
 Map of Shanghai Century Park
 (Click to enlarge) Located on Jinxiu Road in the Pudong New Area of Shanghai, Century Park focuses on lawns, woods, and lakes. The park was designed by the British environmental consultancy LUC and was built in four years from 1996 to 2000. It got its name because it is situated at the end of Century Avenue. Its construction was completed in 2000, the starting point of the new century. The park is known as "Holiday Park" because it is both modern and natural. Numerous different facilities are provided for tourists in the park. They can feed pigeons, go fishing, row a boat, peddle a bicycle, or have fun in the green maze.
With an area of 346 acres (140 hectares), Century Park is divided into seven parts, including the Lakeside Scenic Area, the Forest Landscape Area, the Amenity Grass, the Nature Reserve, the Folk Village, the Exotic Zone, and the Mini Golf Course. There are eight entrances in total, and five of them are open to the public: Gate 1 at the end of Century Avenue, Gate 2 at the crossroad of Jinxiu Road and Minsheng Road, Gate 3 at the crossroad of Jinxiu Road and Jinsong Road, Gate 5 at the crossroad of Huamu Road and Fangdian Road, and Gate 7 at the intersection of Huamu Road and Yinghua Road.
Lakeside Scenic AreaThe major things to do in this area include visiting the Century Clock, Mirror Lake, and the Green World Relief Sculpture Wall.
Century ClockIt is a symbolic scenic spot in Century Park. The three hands of the clock are white, and the twelve graduations are made of Chinese little leaf boxes. Beautiful flowers are arranged around the clock, making it colorful and delicate. The Century Clock is controlled from a satellite, with an error of less than 0.03 second. Thus, it is of high artistic, scientific, and practical value.
Mirror LakeLocated in the west of the park, Mirror Lake is the largest artificial lake in Shanghai. The lake covers a total are of 31 acres (12.5 hectares), and is as deep as 16 feet (5 meters). To the east of the lake is a water lock which controls the water level. Mirror Lake is blue and clean, with the blue sky and white clouds reflected in it. That is very charming! Standing on the wharf by the lake, tourists can see the Lujiazui Financial and Trade Zone in the distance.
Green World Relief Sculpture WallIt presents 29 species of animals and plants from the Asian-Pacific Region. It covers a total area of 213 square yards (178 square meters), and measures 87 yards (80 meters) in length. The animals include China pandas, elephants from Thailand, buffalos from Vietnam, Australian kangaroos, American eagles, and Russian bears. The plants, which vary from tropical plants, semi-tropical plants, temperate plants, to frigid ones, show that creatures adapt themselves to the environment.
Forest Landscape AreaThe main attraction here is the Open-air Music Square. It is situated in the west of Century Park. The square covers a total area of 2 acres (8,000 square meters), with a seating capacity of 2,500 people. This is the largest artificial open-air music square in China.
Amenity GrassLocated in the south of the park, the Amenity Grass is characterized by soft green grass, dotted with European-style buildings with red roof tiles. The Spring Garden, Summer Garden, Autumn Garden, and Winter Garden present tourists with the beauty of all the seasons. Additionally, there is a clear stream flowing by. The scenery is quite fascinating!
Nature ReserveIt mainly includes Birds' Island and the Montreal Garden.
Birds' IslandIt is located at the center of Century Park, with an area of 5 acres (2 hectares). More than 100 magpies are bred here. Moreover, there are more than 50 species of trees on the island, such as peach trees, plum blossom trees, and camphor trees. The tranquil island and the lively birds are in perfect harmony with each other. Tourists will relax and delight in the lovely scenery.
Montreal GardenIt is comprised of islands, lakes, exhibition halls, media rooms, and coffee bars, with a total area of 5 acres (2 hectares). The construction of the park was sponsored by the Canadian Government, the Quebec Provincial Government, and the Montreal Municipal Government, hence its name. The garden is intended to show the harmony among men, nature, science and technology, which is cherished by both Chinese and Canadian people.
Folk VillagePassing through Gate 3, tourists will see the Folk Village in the northeast of the park. The white beach by the limpid lake is surrounded with tall palm trees. Broad Ginkgo Avenue leads to the village. On either side of the avenue are exuberant ginkgo trees which are dozens of years old. In a word, the area brims a fresh pure atmosphere, making tourists comfortable and relaxed.
Exotic ZoneThe highlight of the Exotic Zone lies in the Olmec Colossal Head in the southeast of the Century Park. It was presented to the Shanghai Municipal Government by the Veracruz State Government of Mexico. It is a copy of the eighth original Olmec Head of Mexico. As an embodiment of Mexican civilization, the Olmec culture is characterized by its outstanding carving. The head-shaped stone carving is the well-known piece, and features thick lips, big eyes, and a square face. That looks very splendid!
Mini Golf CourseThe Mini Golf Course in the west end of the park takes up a total area of 8.6 acres (3.5 hectares). There are nine holes on the course for golfers to have fun. The course is also equipped with some barriers, including ponds, shrubs, and bunkers. In addition, coffee bars, golf rental shops, bathrooms, and lounge halls can be found nearby. Golf lovers can have a great time here.
Plum Blossom ShowThe park has a long history of hosting the Plum Blossom Show. At the beginning, some potted plum blossoms were displayed in the Montreal Garden Scenic Area during the Chinese New Year (Spring Festival). That was a small-scale exhibition. Nowadays, there are more than 3,000 plum blossom trees in the park. It is the largest plum blossom woods in Shanghai. Local people as well as tourists often come to the park to enjoy the beautiful and fragrant plum blossoms in winter and spring.
