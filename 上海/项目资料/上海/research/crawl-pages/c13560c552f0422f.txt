The Huangpu River, being the earliest manually cut river, is the mother river of Shanghai, which is the reflection and the symbol of the city. It separates the city into Pudong and Puxi. You can take a cruise on Huangpu River to enjoy its splendid attractions such as the Oriental Pearl TV Tower in Pudong and learn the history and the present of Shanghai. Nanpu Bridge and Yangpu Bridge span the river. Attraction Intro: Huangpu River
