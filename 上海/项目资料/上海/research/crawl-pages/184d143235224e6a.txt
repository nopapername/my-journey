Shanghai Metro
Shanghai Metro Sign  Metro Map 
Shanghai Metro is a speedy, safe, comfortable and convenient means of transportation for traveling around the city. At present, there are 18 Shanghai subway lines in operation. These lines, together with Pujiang Line, Maglev Line, and Airport Link Line, connect the main attractions, transportation hubs and commercial areas such as the Bund, Nanjing Road, Huaihai Road, People's Square, Shanghai Railway Station and Xujiahui. 
On the right is an English-Chinese metro map for your reference, showing the current 21 lines as well as sections and lines under construction or planning. Please click the map to enlarge it. Also you can see the English Version and the Chinese Version, or go for more Metro Maps.

* At Longyang Road Station near the east end of Subway Line 2, the fast Maglev Train (SMT) can take you to Pudong International Airport within eight minutes. The train operates every day from 6:45 to 21:42 at an interval of 15 to 20 minutes. It costs CNY 50 (USD 8) one-way (economic) and CNY 100 (USD 15) for VIP, CNY 80 (USD 12) round-trip (economic) and CNY 160 (USD 24) for VIP.
