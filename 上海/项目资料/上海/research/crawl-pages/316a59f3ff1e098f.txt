Yu Garden is a distinguished classical garden in South China, built during the Jiajing Period of the Ming Dynasty with more than 400 years of history. It is near the City God Temple in the northeast corner of the old city area. The Lantern Festival and Shanghai International Tourism Festival are the busiest time. Floats, colorful lanterns and folk performances always attract parades of people. Attraction Intro: Yu Garden
