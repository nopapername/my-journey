Shanghai Weather in April
 April is definitely one of the best choices for those who are planning to visit Shanghai. The daytime is warm and pleasant and the nighttime is cool. Sunny moments are common and only some short-term drizzles occur from time to time. As for the natural scenery, the color of spring has splashed every inch of the city and all things are reinvigorated.
Warm Daytime and Cool NighttimeApril, a spring month, boasts quite warm and comfortable daytime. The first a few days may still see the daily high of 15-20 degrees Celsius (59-68 degrees Fahrenheit), but since then, the daily high temperature climbs and ranges from around 20°C (68°F) to 27°C (81°F). Occasionally, the city may even experience a hot midday with the high temperature of 30-33°C (86-91°F).
The daily temperature gap is large in Shanghai in April, and it is usually over 10°C (18°F). More specifically, the daily low is mainly in the range of 10-15°C (50-59°F), and occasionally reaches beyond 15°C (59°F) on the last several days of this month. Therefore, on most nights in Shanghai in April, you will still feel a distinct chill in the air. Cozy Daytime of April  
Frequent but Light and Short-duration RainThe overall precipitation is at a moderate level of 130 mm on average in April. The number of rainy days, which is 15 on average, is one of the largest throughout the year. 
However, actually, the rain won’t produce much inconvenience for your tour. According to the historical records, the rain this month is usually light in intensity and short in duration.
As for the humidity, it averages 69% this month, and the air is a bit damp. Thanks to the pleasant temperature, the relatively high humidity will not cause an uncomfortable feeling. Sunny View of Shanghai in April  
Long Daylight Hours & Strong UV RaysThe early sunrise at around 05:11-05:43 and late sunset at 18:13-18:33 contribute to another favorable weather condition for traveling around: long daytime. In general, the daylight hours stretch from 12.3 to 13.2 from the beginning to the end of the month. There is no need to rush during the sightseeing, even if you have a relatively packed schedule. 
Sunshine hours average 5 this month. You should be especially careful about the strong sunlight. At midday, the UV index can reach as high as 9, and if you are exposed to this sun light for a long time without taking protection measures, your skin may get sunburned easily.

What to Pack When Visiting Shanghai in April? Clothes: For the warm daytime, outfits like long-sleeved T-shirts plus sweatpants, short-sleeved T-shirts and shirts plus jeans, and long-sleeved dresses plus thin leggings are all suitable. On some sunny midday hours in late April, even T-shirts plus shorts or skirts are OK to wear. With huge temperature differences between day and night, after sunset, trench coats, leather jackets, or denim jackets are needed.
Shoes: Sneakers, canvas shoes, and loafers are all good choices, as long as they are comfortable enough.
Others: 
1. During sunny daytime, sun-protection items, including sunglasses, hats, and sunscreen, are essential.
2. An umbrella should be put into your bag for possible rain.
3. This month is the pollen season, and those with rhinitis are recommended to prepare some masks.
What to See When Visiting Shanghai in April?In warm April with many sunny hours, you will find it a very pleasant experience whether you are visiting the Yu Garden to admire the traditional Chinese garden architecture, heading to the Bund to take in the night views, or strolling down Nanjing Road to experience the bustling view of Shanghai. In addition, following special highlights in April may deliver you more choices and a richer experience. Dazzling Night View  
1. Lively Festivals(1) Longhua Temple Fair
Each year, the temple fair is held on the 3rd day of the 3rd lunar month, which is usually in April. During the temple fair, folk performances can be admired, souvenirs can be bought in the cultural and creative products market, and delicious local food can be tasted. In 2027, it will take place on April 9 of the Gregorian calendar.
(2) Formula 1 Chinese Grand Prix
It has been held at the Shanghai International Circuit since 2004 and is a key Asian stop on the F1 calendar. It is typically held in April each year. If you’re a motorsports fan, this is definitely an event you won’t want to miss.
2. Vibrant Spring ViewIn April, Shanghai looks like a giant garden. You will find charming spring views in Yu Garden, Zhujiajiao Water Town, Century Park, the Shanghai Botanical Garden, People’s Park, Jing’an Sculpture Park, etc., which are all bursting with colorful flowers and lush green trees. 
 Street View of Shanghai in April Spring View of the Yu Garden 
Is Shanghai Crowded in April?April indeed sees a growing number of visitors due to the overall pleasant weather of Shanghai and beautiful scenery. Accordingly, famous attractions like the Yuyuan Garden and the Bund will be a little crowded, and hotel prices will grow by much.
Pay special attention to the fact that the first and last a few days of the month will be much more crowded than usual: The Qingming Festival falls on April 4 or 5, and a three-day public holiday is given; and at the end of April, many Chinese will be arriving in Shanghai to start their May Day holiday.
Tips for Shanghai Trips in April1. For trips to Shanghai this month with many tourists, early booking, for both attractions and hotels, is the top priority, especially if your traveling dates are in early or late April, two periods that are actually advised to avoid. 
2. The climate of Shanghai in April is kind of changeable, so remember to check the weather forecast from time to time. On rainy days, you are advised to adjust your itinerary flexibly and add some indoor activities like visiting the Shanghai Museum and the Shanghai Science and Technology Museum, and hang around in malls.
