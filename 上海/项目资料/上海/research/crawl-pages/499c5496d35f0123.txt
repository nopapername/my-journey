Tianzifang is situated in Lane 210, Taikang Road, Shanghai. Before 1998, it was a noisy street market. At present, under the help of local government, it has developped into an art street with many studios, craftwork shops, design shops, galleries and cafes. The biggest feature of this place is that many residents still live in the narrow lanes and shikumen buildings. Attraction Intro: Tianzifang
