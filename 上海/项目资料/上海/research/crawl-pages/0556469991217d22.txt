Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport
Airport Code: SHA 
 Location: Hongqiao International Airport, abbreviated as SHA airport, is located at No.2550, Hongqiao Road, Changning District;
about 13 kilometers (8 miles) from the downtown area and 60 kilometers (37 miles) from Pudong Airport.
 Two Terminals: T1 for domestic and international flights; T2 for domestic flights
 Hongqiao Airport Maps 
Passenger Guide: 
1. Time for Taking Flights
Arrive at the airport 2-3.5 hours before flight departure
Closing Time for Check-in: Domestic Flight: 40 mins. before takeoff; International Flight: 45 mins. before takeoff
Closing Time for Boarding: Domestic & International Flight: 15 mins. before take-off
 Self-service Machines 
2. Arrival & Departure Procedures in T1 & T2 of Hongqiao Airport
International Arrival: Inspection & Quarantine – Immigration Inspection – Luggage Claim – Customs – Leave the Airport
Domestic Arrival: Luggage Claim – Leave the Airport
International Departure: Check-in – Inspection & Quarantine – Customs – Immigration – Security Check – Waiting & Boarding
Domestic Departure: Check-in – Security Check – Waiting & Boarding
3. Transfer in Hongqiao Airport
 Minimum Connection Time: D refers to Domestic; I refers to International
Transfer in the Same Terminal: D ⇒ D: 1 hour, D ⇔ I: 1.5 hours
Transfer from T2 to T1: D ⇒ D: 2.5 hours, D ⇔ I: 3 hours
Guide Map of Transfer Procedures of T2
4. Transfer between Hongqiao Airport & Pudong Airport
 Minimum Connection Time: Get on the shuttle bus/metro 3 hours ahead of time for domestic flights; 4 hours ahead for international flights
 Prior Transportation Choices: Suburban Railway Airport Link Line & Shuttle Bus Line 1 & Metro Line 2 
 See more: How to Get from Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station to Pudong Airport 
5. How to Travel from Hongqiao Airport to Hongqiao Railway Station
From T1: Beyond walking distance; take metro line 10
From T2: Walk around 600 meters (656 yards), 10 min.

The policy is applicable to citizens of 54 countries. Passengers are allowed to fly in and fly out from Pudong or Hongqiao Airport in Shanghai and other 23 provinces and regions and cross provincial boundaries to explore most tourist cities within the 240-hour time limit.
