Europe-Joint Pavilion
The Shanghai World Expo Site is located between the Nanpu Bridge and Lupu Bridge, and occupies the waterfront on both sides of the Huangpu River. It attracts 242 countries and international organizations. With its theme "Better City, Better Life", it manifests in three features: universality, originality and modernity. The unique designs of the pavilions of the 45 European Countries are really appealing to visitors. Expo Intro: Expo 2010 Shanghai
