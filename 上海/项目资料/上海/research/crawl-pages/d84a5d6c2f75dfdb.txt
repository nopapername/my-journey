As a quiet place in the noisy city, Jingan Temple attracts many people all the year round. Many people go there praying for a good future for their career and education. The jade statue of Buddha is larger than that in Jade Buddha Temple. Also, Jingan Temple contains hundreds of cultural relics such as the famous painting by Zhu Da, a famous painter of the Ming Dynasty (1368-1644). Attraction Information: Jing'an Temple
