Shanghai Disneyland Park
Shanghai Disneyland Park is the core wonderland inside the Disney Resort in Chuansha New Town of Pudong New Area. It is the first in Chinese mainland and the sixth over the globe, featuring the tallest castle (69 meters/75 yards)—Enchanted Storybook Castle—in the world. As the newest and most modern Disneyland park worldwide, it not only inherits the essence of Disney by creating a dreamy fairy tale kingdom for visitors through classic amusement facilities such as “Peter Pan’s Flight” in Fantasyland, “Pirates of the Caribbean” in Treasure Cove, as well as the parade of floats, but also boasts the world’s first Zootopia theme land and incorporates distinctive Chinese cultural elements, including Disney characters corresponding to the Chinese zodiac, Disney characters in traditional Chinese costume, classic Disney musicals with elements of Chinese Peking Opera, etc. All of these makes Shanghai Disneyland Park unique among all Disney parks across the world.
Top 10 Rides in Shanghai Disneyland Park
1. Zootopia: Hot PursuitArea: Zootopia
Height requirement: 81cm (2.7 feet) or taller
Feature: Here you become a new police officer in Zootopia as you ride in a police car and join Judy and Nick in chasing after the mastermind villain. Powered by AR dynamic racing and a fully immersive 360° dome screen, you’ll race from Glacier Town through Sahara Square, then dash across the Rainforest Runway, fully immersed in the tightly woven, thrilling plot of a wildly vibrant animal metropolis. Amusement in Zootopia  
2. Soaring Over the HorizonArea: Adventure Isle
Height requirement: 102 cm (3.3 feet) or taller
Feature: This is a 5D global flight experience that combines visual, auditory and olfactory sensations. During this 5-minute journey, you will soar, glide and circle at high altitudes above more than a dozen world-class landmarks such as the Alps, African savannah, the Great Wall of China, the Pyramids of Egypt, the Taj Mahal in India, the Eiffel Tower in Paris, and the Sydney Opera House, etc. At the same time, each scene is accompanied by a grand symphony and specific scents, which feels so real.
3. Tron Lightcycle Power RunArea: Tomorrowland
Height requirement: 122 cm (4 feet) or taller
Feature: Riding in one of Disney’s fastest roller coasters, you transform into a “lightcycle warrior” as you blast through a neon-lit cyberpunk realm with thumping electronic beats and conquer this 966-meter (0.6 miles) futuristic track with high-speed turns, drops, and surges that keep your excitement peaking the entire ride. Tomorrowland  
4. Seven Dwarfs Mine TrainArea: Fantasyland
Height requirement: 97 cm (3.2 feet) or taller
Feature: Just as if you were really riding in a mine cart, you can feel the tense yet amusing jolts all the way. Driving through this series of undulating mines, you first enter a cave filled with colorful gemstones, then encounter seven little dwarfs who are whistling and singing while mining, and finally cross the waterfall and arrive at the cottage where the little dwarfs and Snow White are dancing happily. Seven Dwarfs Mine Train Roaring Rapids   
5. Roaring RapidsArea: Adventure Isle
Height requirement: 107 cm (3.5 feet) or taller
Feature: This is a “perilous” water adventure: getting on the round raft and floating downstream, you will be tossed in the rapids and sent swirling around. And then you are carried into a cave where giant beasts reside. As the roar of the beasts fades away, you will fall into a lava tunnel, then be caught in a turbulent whirlpool, and finally, led by the rapids back to the base camp, completing the adventure challenge and becoming a fearless explorer.
6. Pirates of the Caribbean – Battle for the Sunken TreasureArea: Treasure Cove
Height requirement: 81 cm (2.7 feet) or taller
Feature: With top-notch audio-visual effects and faithful restoration of classic characters in the film, you immediately immerse in the scene as you board the pirate ship and sail slowly through the waters. Be cautious! A spectacular and thrilling adventure of sea battle and treasure hunt is awaiting ahead. Can you break through the pirates’ encirclement in the dim cave, then follow Captain Jack to dive into the underwater world and fight with the fierce Davy Jones, and finally succeed in seizing the treasure?  Pirate Ship in Treasure Cove  Entrance of Pirates of Caribbean  
7. Rex's RacerArea: Disney Pixar Toy Story Land
Height requirement: 120 cm (3.9 feet) or taller
Feature: Once you’re seated on the 90° vertical U-shaped track, you’ve become one of Andy’s toys, controlled by Rex the dinosaur with his remote control. Get ready for thrilling high-speed pendulum swings and nonstop moments of weightlessness!
8. Woody's RoundupArea: Disney Pixar Toy Story Land
Height requirement: 81 cm (2.7 feet) or taller
Feature: Hop onto the nostalgic Western carriage, and let the little ponies carry you through Woody and Jessie’s cowboy hoedown, swaying and spinning to cheerful music beats. The intensity of the swings can be mild or wild — it’s up to you! Fantasyland  
9. Peter Pan's FlightArea: Fantasyland
Height requirement: 81 cm (2.7 feet) or taller
Feature: You board the suspended flying ship and experience the true feeling of soaring in the dark track. The ascent, descent, gliding and turning throughout the journey are relatively gentle, allowing you to relax as you journey through the classic scenes of “Peter Pan” beneath the romantic and mysterious sky glowing with moonlight and stars. Starting from the London night sky to the Neverland for an adventure, and then closely observing the sword duel between Peter Pan and Captain Hook, you return safely in the end.
10. Challenge Trails at Camp Discovery Rope Walk Challenge in Adventure Isle Area: Adventure Isle
Height requirement: 106 cm (3.5 feet) or taller
Feature: This is the Rope Walk Challenge. You need to cross a series of obstacles high above the ground to reach the destination. The entire journey takes approximately 20 minutes. There are three thrilling routes—the Hidden Falls Chamber, the Echo Cavern, and the House of the Ancients—each offering distinctive and splendid views along the way.
Recommendations for Family Tourists with Kids
1. Voyage to the Crystal GrottoArea: Fantasyland
Feature: This is the pier that belongs exclusively to the Fantasyland. As you and your children ride the outdoor cruise ship and meander along, a series of lifelike statues of fairy tale characters from “Beauty and the Beast”, “Aladdin”, “Fantasia”, “Enchanted”, “Mulan”, and “The Little Mermaid” pass by. Amidst the changing colors and the beautiful melody, the classic story scenes of Disney are replayed before your eyes one after another. When the night falls, this whole magical land will become even more fabulous, enhanced by stunning light effects. Voyage to the Crystal Grotto  
2. Enchanted Storybook Castle Relief of Disney Princess in the Castle Area: Fantasyland
Feature: The Enchanted Storybook Castle is more than just the landmark of Shanghai Disneyland. It is the first castle in the world to bring together all Disney Princesses, fulfilling every little girl’s princess dream. As you step inside and climb up the grand staircase, classic Disney princess images unfold one by one before your eyes. Nighttime is when magic reaches its peak. The castle transforms into a giant projection screen, with fireworks, lasers, flames, fountains and dynamic music all coming together. Fairytale moments are frozen in time here.
3. Alice in Wonderland MazeArea: Fantasyland
Feature: This is an outdoor maze attraction. You can choose one of the three mysterious doors to enter the fascinating land. Each door leads to a different scene from Alice in Wonderland, and eventually reaches the Mad Hatter’s Tea Party. Which door will you pick to begin this interesting exploration journey?
4. The Many Adventures of Winnie the PoohArea: Fantasyland
Feature: By sitting inside the honey pot vehicle and gliding smoothly along the track, you wander through the forest to the soothing music, reliving the warm and touching fairy tales of Winnie the Pooh and his friends.
5. Hunny Pot Spin Hunny Hot Spin in Fantasyland Area: Fantasyland
Feature: Here are 18 honey pots rotating along with the large turntable. You can also adjust the rotation speed of the pot itself by turning the middle dial inside. It’s very easy and fun, perfect for children.
6. Dumbo the Flying ElephantArea: Gardens of Imagination
Feature: Without thrilling rise and fall, this ride offers you a leisurely flight as you sit on the back of a lovely elephant.
Unmissable Disney Performances
1. Mickey's Storybook ExpressTime: 12:15; 15:45
Feature: This is a 14-minute mobile film extravaganza that is renowned worldwide for its longest route. Mickey drives the train and classic film characters make their appearances, creating an atmosphere of pure joy.
2. ILLUMINATE! A Nighttime CelebrationTime: 20:00; 21:15
Feature: The high-precision projection technology turns the entire castle into a dynamic canvas. Classic characters from Disney, Pixar, and Marvel have all come on stage. Combined with fireworks, lights, fountains, and music, it creates a dreamy fairy tale night. Mickey’s Storybook Express  
3. Eye of the Storm: Captain Jack's Stunt SpectacularDuration: 30 min
Feature: At the Theater Fandango, a fake Captain Jack is performing when the real Captain Jack suddenly descends from above, sparking an epic battle against the British Royal Navy filled with clashes and treasure hunts. The show blends pirate storytelling with thrilling stunts, hilarious comedy, and interactive moments.
4. Frozen: A Sing-Along CelebrationDuration: 20 min
Feature: This interactive musical show retells the touching story of Frozen. While performers invite the audience to sing along to original songs of the film, beautiful snow effects fill the whole stage, impressing everyone’s heart. Entrance of Theater Fandango Stage Performance  
5. Micky's Storybook AdventureDuration: 28 min
Feature: At the Walt Disney Theatre, with “sending Olaf home” as the thread, Broadway-level singing and dancing paired with an immersive stage weave together 13 classic Disney stories, 23 Disney stars and 10 golden songs, creating a visual and auditory feast.
Other performances like The Heart of Magic, FriendSHIP! Duffy and Friends’ Special Pre-Parade, Magic Together: A Special 10th Birthday Celebration, Disney Color-Fest: A Street Party, Chip & Dale Celebration, A Special Celebration in the Hundred Acre Wood, etc. are also brilliant. If you are going to stay in Shanghai Disneyland Park for more days, you may enjoy them as you like. Micky’s Storybook Adventure 
NOTE: These performances may have their show times adjusted slightly based on the day’s circumstances. It is recommended to refer to the performance schedule of the park in real time.

Does Shanghai Disneyland Park Have Priority Passes?Yes. For tourists who want to start your itinerary earlier instead of spending more time waiting in line, there are various types of Disney FastPass ticket provided, including Early Park Entry Pass, Disney Premier Access, and Bundle Ticket that comprises several amusement rides with priority entry. You need to download the official application of Shanghai Disney Resort, and purchase the FastPass tickets there.

Where to Stay near Shanghai Disneyland Park?
Inside the Shanghai Disney Resort Map of Shanghai Disneyland Park () 
  Pictures  There are two upscale hotels inside the Disney Resort, Shanghai Disneyland Hotel and Toy Story Hotel, which all feature classic Disney elements, from the main hall, corridor, restaurants, to guest rooms and other entertainment facilities. In addition, both hotels offer free shuttle bus services so you don’t have to bother walking to reach the park. Best of all, staying in one of the two hotels allows you to enter the park one hour in advance in the morning through early access passage. This way, you can avoid the peak of crowded queues and taking advantage of the time difference to enjoy the most popular rides first. If you feel tired halfway while playing, you may go back to the hotel to have a comfortable rest, which is very convenient.
Outside the Shanghai Disney ResortYou can also opt to stay overnight in hotels outside the Shanghai Disney Resort, which are much cheaper and thus suitable for budget travelers. However, it might take you a longer time to enter the park, which could lead to encountering peak queuing hours.

How to Get to Disneyland Park Shanghai?
From Shanghai Downtown1. Take Shanghai Metro Line 11 and get off at Disneyland Park Station. Then, go out via Exit 1 or 4, and walk for about 5 minutes to the park.
2. Take Shanghai Bus No. 50, 51, 52, or 70, and get off at West Bus Hub or the Qimiaolu Shuttle Station. Then walk for a few minutes to enter the park.
From Shanghai AirportsShanghai Pudong International Airport to Shanghai Disneyland Park
1. Take a taxi for 30-50 minutes at the cost of around CNY 100-140 (USD 14-22) to the park.
2. Take Shanghai Metro Line 2, transfer to Line 16, then Line 11 to the Disneyland Park Station. You will see the park right in your line of sight.
Shanghai Hongqiao International Airport to Shanghai Disneyland Park
1. Take a taxi for 60-80 minutes at the cost of around CNY 160-210 (USD 23-32) to the park.
2. Take Shanghai Metro Line 2 or Line 10, and then transfer to Line 11 to the Disneyland Park Station. Disneyland Park Station 
From HangzhouTake a bullet train from Hangzhou East Railway Station to Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station for about one hour and at the cost of CNY 52-82 (USD 7-12). Then take Shanghai Metro Line 10 to Jiaotong University Station, and transfer to Line 11 to the Disneyland Park Station. Go out via Exit 1 and follow the direction boards to the park.

Friendly Reminder1. Visitors aged 16 and above are not allowed to wear costumes of characters in cartoons, movies and comics. Floor-length garments are also prohibited. Besides, tourists under the age of 16 must be accompanied by a tourist who is 16 years old or above when entering the park.
2. Some of the amusement facilities in the park are quite intense and may not be suitable for visitors with heart diseases, high blood pressure, etc. Besides, most of them have height restrictions. Therefore, it is recommended that you check the requirements before playing so as to avoid waiting in line unnecessarily.
3. You need to notice that glass bottles, heated food such as instant noodles and self-heating food, and food with pungent odors like durian, etc. are no allowed to bring.
4. Other prohibited items include selfie sticks, large tripods, and folding chairs.
Practical Tips for Traveling in Shanghai Disneyland Park1. The park offers a wide variety of restaurants serving Chinese, Western, and fusion cuisine. There are also diversified eateries in each themed land, though prices are quite high. You may bring your own food such as bread and cookies — it’s more economical and saves time so you can enjoy more rides.
2. There are luggage lockers available at the Disneyland Park subway station and near the security check point of the park.
3. You can download the official application of Shanghai Disney Resort beforehand so as to monitor the duration needed for queuing for your desired rides, and then plan your ride order wisely to avoid wasting extra time. Shanghai Disneyland Crowds in Peak Season 
4. Some rides, such as Zootopia: Hot Pursuit and Seven Dwarfs Mine Train, offer a free single-rider line based on crowd levels. This line usually moves faster, but you cannot choose your seat — staff will assign you to any available spot. It is ideal for solo visitors or groups prioritizing ride efficiency over sitting together.
5. Wheelchair and pram rental services (each costing CNY 90 / USD 13 per day) are available both inside and outside the park. These services are particularly suitable for tourists with mobility issues or those with little kids. By the way, they can also store some items that need to be carried with you, thus reducing your burden.
6. It's suggested that you avoid periods like May Day Holiday from May 1st to 5th, National Day holiday from October 1st to 7th and summer holiday from mid-July to late August when the park tends to be very crowded. If you are coming to visit the park during summer season (June-August), be sure to bring along sun protection and heatstroke prevention gear, such as sun hats, parasols, portable fans, cooling patches, etc. in case you get sunburned due to long queues outdoors.
7. If you plan to go on water rides, better bring your own raincoat—it’s much cheaper and will keep you from feeling uncomfortable in wet clothes.
8. Considering that you need to walk a lot around the park, remember to wear comfortable shoes.
9. The amusement facilities and outdoor performances may be postponed or closed due to weather conditions and other unforeseen factors. It is recommended to check the official website of Shanghai Disneyland Park for real-time information.
