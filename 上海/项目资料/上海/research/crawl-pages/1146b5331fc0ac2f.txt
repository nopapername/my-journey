The Oriental Pearl Tower is a cultural symbol and a landmark of Shanghai. With a height of 1,536 feet (468 meters), the tower is the world's sixth and China's second tallest TV and radio tower. Inside the tower, there is also a Shanghai Municipal History Museum, exhibiting the past of Shanghai; a sightseeing area for tourists to overview Shanghai; and a revolving restaurant, where one can appreciate amazing scenery while enjoying delicious food.
