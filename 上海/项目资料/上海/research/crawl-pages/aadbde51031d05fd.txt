Shanghai Pudong International Airport
 Operating flights to 250+ domestic and international destinations, Shanghai Pudong Airport, abbreviated as PVG airport, is one of China's most important air traffic hubs.
Airport Code: PVG
 Location: No.6000, Yingbin Avenue, Pudong New Area; 30 kilometers (19 miles) from the downtown area and 40 kilometers (25 miles) from Hongqiao Airport.
 Two Terminals: T1, T2
 Pudong Airport Maps 
Passenger Guide 
1. Time for Taking FlightsArrive at the airport 2-3.5 hours before flight departure
Closing Time for Check-in: Domestic Flight: 45 mins. before take-off; International Flight: 50 mins. before take-off
Closing Time for Boarding: Domestic & International Flight: 15 mins. before take-off
2. Arrival & Departure Procedures at T1 & T2 in Pudong AirportInternational Arrival: Inspection & Quarantine – Immigration Inspection – Luggage Claim – Customs – Leaving the Airport
Domestic Arrival: Luggage Claim – Leaving the airport
International Departure: Check-in – Inspection & Quarantine – Customs – Immigration – Security Check – Waiting & Boarding
Domestic Departure: Check-in – Security Check - Waiting & Boarding
3. How to Travel Between Pudong Airport & Downtown Shanghai
(↓details underneath): Airport Shuttle Bus, Maglev + Metro, Metro Line 2, Taxi
 See more How to Get from Shanghai Hongqiao Railway Station to Pudong Airport 
4. Transfer in Pudong Airport Minimum Connection Time: D refers to Domestic; I refers to International
Transfer in the Same Terminal: D ⇒ D: 80 mins.  D ⇔ I: 90 mins.   I ⇒ I: 100 mins
Transfer between Two Terminals: D ⇒ D: 120 mins.   D ⇔ I: 160 mins.   I ⇒ I: 120 mins.

Facilities & Services of Shanghai Pudong Airport
 240-Hour Visa-Free Transit:
Citizens of 54 countries and regions can make an air transit in Pudong or Hongqiao Airports without a visa if the period of stay does not exceed 240 hours. Except for the two airports, there are other 58 eligible ports including airports, ferry ports and railway ports across 23 provinces and regions people can enter through and travel between the provinces and regions.
