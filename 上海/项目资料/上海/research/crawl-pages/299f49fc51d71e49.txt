The Ocean Aquarium is located at 1388 Lujiazui Ring Road, Pudong New Area. It is adjacent to the Oriental Pearl TV Tower. As one of the largest ocean aquariums in the world, it is divided into 10 exhibition areas: China Area, South America Area, Australia Area, Africa Area, Southeast Asia Area, Cold Water Area, Polar Area, Sea and Shore Area, Deep Ocean Area and Special Exihibition. It has 12,000 kinds of rare fish and endangered living creatures on display. Attraction Intro: Shanghai Ocean Aquarium
