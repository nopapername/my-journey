Shanghai Weather in March
 In March, spring weather warms up the whole city of Shanghai. Some drizzles fall, bringing the vibrant spring picture: the air is fresh and clean, trees like willows bud, flowers like cherry blossoms and tulips gradually bloom… Nice weather and bright spring scenery make March a definitely good month to visit Shanghai.
TemperatureAn overall increasing trend can be noticed throughout March. In the first half of the month, still a little cold days with the daily high and low of around 10-15 degrees Celsius (50-59 degrees Fahrenheit) and 5-10°C (41-50°F), respectively, are more common. In the second half month, warm days, which enjoy daytime temperature of around 15-25°C (59-77°F) and nighttime temperature of 10-15°C (50-59°F), show up more frequently.  Yu Garden in Cold March 
However, you cannot decide what to pack for your trip based only on the above overall temperature picture: instability is also an obvious feature of the temperature of Shanghai in March that cannot be overlooked. On the one hand, the temperature gap between day and night can be quite large, which may be over 15°C (27°F) sometimes. For instance, on March 2, 2025, the daytime was warm or even a bit hot at around 28°C (82°F), while the nighttime brought cold air with the temperature of only 10°C (50°F). On the other hand, temperature differences between two consecutive days can also be big, which are demonstrated by sudden temperature drops or rises of around 5-15°C (9-27°F).
Precipitation & HumidityAbout 14 rainy days show up in March on average, which is relatively frequent, but the average monthly precipitation is not high at only 84 mm. This indicates that drizzle is the main type of rain this month. What is also favorable for your trip is that drizzles usually stop quickly, followed by gentle sunshine. 
Shanghai suffers from wet and damp air in March, and the humidity is about 70% on average. You will feel even colder when the temperature is low, due to the high humidity.
 The Bund on Sunny Days Clear Sky in March  
Daylight Hours & Sunshine HoursMarch in Shanghai gets gradually extending daytime from about 11.3 to 12.3 hours, so you don’t need to worry that you cannot gain enough daylight for walking around, and just slow down and have a leisurely trip, especially in the second half of the month. Besides, with relatively early sunset time at 17:51-18:12, you can enjoy illuminated night views early.
Plenty of sunshine guarantees good visibility and light conditions for outdoor visiting and photographing: sunshine hours per day average about 5 hours this month. During the midday hours of sunny days, when the UV index can reach 7, sun-protection measures are essential, especially for those having sensitive skin.

One of the Best Months to Visit ShanghaiMarch is considered one of the best months to visit Shanghai and trips to the Bund, the Yu Garden, the Nanjing Road, etc., will all reward you with perfect experiences:
1. Temperature-wise, Shanghai is gradually getting rid of the damp and chilly weather and becoming warmer and warmer, while sudden temperature drops take place only occasionally.
2. Rain, though a bit frequently, is usually light and short, guaranteeing plenty of sunshine.
3. The visitor volume is moderate this month, so you can book nice hotel rooms at budget-friendly prices and visit attractions at a leisurely pace without waiting in lines for a long period.
4. In March, Shanghai is ablaze with vibrant spring colors, as bright flowers bloom everywhere.  Disneyland Park Full of Flowers in March  
What to Wear in Shanghai in March 2027? Clothes: The weather of Shanghai in March is changeable, so you need to prepare clothes of different thicknesses. On most days in the first half of the month, and on some days in the second half of the month when Shanghai is hit by cold air, you should wear hoodies, thick sweatpants, and down jackets or wool coats. On most days in the second half month, you can change the down jackets or wool coats into 3-in-1 jackets or windbreakers. Sometimes, when the daytime temperature is over 25°C (77°F), even a trench coat or an outer layer of a 3-in-1 jacket alone is enough.
Shoes: Choose a pair of comfortable and relatively thick sneakers, walking shoes, or boots.
Others: 
1. Never forget to bring an umbrella with you when traveling to Shanghai in March.
2. Sun-protection items like sunglasses, hats, and sunscreen are better to prepare.
3. People who are allergic to pollen are advised to wear masks
Tips for Visiting Shanghai in March1. As mentioned above, March will not see very high visitor flow in Shanghai, and the Shanghai Disneyland Park also welcomes its rare slack season. You will indeed have a smoother trip that requires a relatively short time to queue; however, you should know that due to the decline in visitor numbers, the Park will close early, and the number of fireworks shows and parade performances will be reduced. If you plan to visit it this month, please check the related information in advance.
2. Try to arrange your travel on weekdays. In this month of flower blooming, weekends are the time for the locals to spring out, indicating that parks and public transportation will be crowded.
3. The climate of Shanghai in March is quite changeable, making it important to check the weather forecast from time to time. Shanghai Spring View 
4. Want to see flowers to feel the spring? Yu Garden provides pink cherry blossoms and white magnolias, and the area around the Oriental Pearl Tower has white magnolias and tulips to see. Or, if time permits, Gu Village Park, boasting 16,000 trees and a lively cherry blossom festival, and Jing’an Sculpture Park in the city center, with large-scale cherry blossoms and tulips, are recommended destinations to visit.
